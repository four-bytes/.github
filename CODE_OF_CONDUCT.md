# Code of Conduct

All Four Bytes projects follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, welcome people of all backgrounds, focus on what is best for
the community, and accept that maintainers may remove comments, commits, issues or contributors that
violate these standards.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to **info@4bytes.de**. All reports are reviewed and handled
confidentially. Enforcement follows the guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
